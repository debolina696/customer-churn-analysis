## 📑 Workflow
1. **Data Cleaning**  
   Handle missing values, outliers, categorical encoding, scaling.

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**  
   Visualize churn distribution, customer demographics, service usage.

3. **Feature Engineering**  
   Create tenure groups, service bundles, payment behavior flags.

4. **Modeling**  
   Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost with hyperparameter tuning.

5. **Evaluation**  
   Confusion matrix, ROC-AUC, precision-recall, feature importance.

6. **Power BI Dashboard**  
   Professional, advanced insights for executives and analysts.

---

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard Highlights
- **Executive Overview**: Overall churn rate, revenue impact, CLV distribution  
- **Customer Segmentation**: Churn by demographics, tenure, service bundles  
- **Behavioral Insights**: Payment methods, contract types, usage patterns  
- **Predictive Modeling**: Churn probability, top risk factors, customer risk scores  
- **Retention Strategy Simulation**: What-if analysis for reducing churn  

---

## 🚀 Business Impact
- Identify high-risk customers early  
- Design targeted retention strategies  
- Quantify revenue impact of churn reduction  
- Provide executives with clear, actionable insights

- ## 📌 Key Results
- Logistic Regression achieved 82% accuracy.
- Tenure length, contract type, and payment method were the strongest churn predictors.
- Power BI dashboard highlights high‑risk customer segments for proactive retention.
